How an NFC card works

NFC is the same tiny technology behind tap-to-pay. We program a link on the chip inside your card. Anyone with a modern phone can open it with a tap.

  1. Choose your card

    Pick a card or sticker, tell us the link you want: Instagram, WhatsApp, Google review, UPI or any website.

  2. We program and test it

    We write your link on the NFC chip, test it on Android and iPhone, then ship it to you with India Post.

  3. Tap and your link opens

    Your customer taps their phone on the card and your link opens instantly. You can change the link anytime.

Questions we often hear

Do I need an app to use the card?

No. Modern Android phones and iPhones read NFC cards without any app. The phone simply opens your link.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. iPhone 7 and newer open NFC links by tapping the top of the phone on the card. Most Android phones work too. If NFC is off on Android, turn it on in Settings.

Can I change the link later?

Yes. The link on the chip can be rewritten. Message us in the order chat and we will guide you.

How do I order?

Tap Buy now on a card, login with Google, and send your order to our chat. We confirm your address and payment details there.

How does payment work?

For fewer than 10 cards, pay the full amount first. For 10 or more, pay 50% in advance and the rest before shipping. We verify every payment before we continue.

Can I get a refund?

Once payment is made, the amount is not refundable. Every card has a 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects, and we replace faulty cards under that warranty.

How long does delivery take?

We ship with India Post after your card is programmed and tested. You get the tracking ID in your order chat so you can follow the parcel.

I opened this link from Instagram and login does not work. What now?

Google blocks login inside the Instagram browser. Open this page in Chrome or Safari, or use the email link login option.
