Warranty

Last updated: 1 November 2026. (Update this date whenever you change this page.)

Every NFC Trust card comes with a 3-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

What is covered

  • NFC chip that stops working because of a manufacturing defect.
  • Printing defects that are not caused by use.
  • Cards that arrive damaged or not working.

What is not covered

  • Cards that are bent, cut, cracked, burnt or broken.
  • Water damage on cards that are not water-resistant.
  • Wear from normal daily use, such as small scratches or fading.
  • Cards used with a metal case or metal surface that blocks NFC (except metal cards made for this).
  • Phones that do not support NFC or have NFC turned off.

How to claim

  1. Login on our website and open your order chat (or the Help chat).
  2. Send your Order ID and a short video or photo showing the problem.
  3. We check the card. If it is covered, we repair or replace it.

Warranty starts from the date your order is delivered. Warranty gives you a repair or replacement. It does not give a money refund. See the Refund Policy.
