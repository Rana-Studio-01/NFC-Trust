Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. (Update this date whenever you change this page.)

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what we collect when you use NFC Trust and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Login details: when you login with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo. If you use the email link option, we receive your email address.
  • Order and chat data: the cards you order, messages you send, images you attach (such as a payment screenshot or design) and details you share in chat such as your delivery address and phone number.
  • Reviews: the rating and text you write, with your name and a partly hidden email.
  • Visit count: we count each browser once to show the visitor number. This does not identify you.

2. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your order and talk to you about it.
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud.
  • To show your review on our website.
  • To protect the website from abuse and spam.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Google processes it on our behalf under its own security and privacy terms.

4. Who can see your data

  • Only you and the NFC Trust owner can read your order chats and help chats.
  • In our admin panel your email is hidden by default and shown only when we need it for your order.
  • Reviews are public, but only with a partly hidden email.

5. Browser storage

We store small items in your browser, such as whether you have seen the welcome message, a short-lived cache of products and your login session. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. Shipping information

Your address and phone number are used only to ship your order. We may share them with India Post when we send your parcel.

7. Your choices

You can logout at any time from the menu. To ask us to delete your account, chats or review, contact us using the details below and we will help you.

8. Children

Our website is meant for adults and for children only with a parent's help. Do not share personal details of a child without a parent's permission.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions? Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram.
