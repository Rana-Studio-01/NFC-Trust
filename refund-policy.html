Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. (Update this date whenever you change this page.)

Once payment is made, the amount is not refundable. Please check your order, card type, quantity and link carefully before you pay.

1. No refund after payment

We start working on your order as soon as your payment is verified. Because we program, test and prepare each card for you, we cannot return money after a payment has been made. This applies to advance payments, balance payments and full payments.

2. Before you pay

  • You can change the cards or quantity in your order chat before payment starts.
  • You can cancel the order at no cost any time before you make a payment. Just tell us in the order chat.

3. Faulty or damaged cards

If your card does not work because of a manufacturing defect, or arrives damaged, we will repair or replace it. This is covered by our 3-year warranty. See the Warranty page. Please contact us in your order chat with your Order ID and a short video or photo.

4. Bulk orders

For orders of 10 or more cards, the 50% advance is also non-refundable once paid. The balance must be paid before shipping.

5. Custom cards

Custom designs are made only for you. They cannot be cancelled or refunded once printing or programming has started.

6. Contact

For any problem with an order, message us in your order chat or on WhatsApp.
