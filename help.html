Help Center

Everything you need to order, track and use your NFC card.

How do I order?

  • Open the website and choose a card. Tap Buy now and confirm.
  • Login with Google when asked. It takes one tap and creates your account automatically.
  • Add more cards or change the quantity with the Add another card button, then tap Send order to chat.
  • We reply in your order chat, confirm your address and share payment details.

How does payment work?

Fewer than 10 cards: pay the full amount first. 10 or more: pay 50% in advance and the rest before shipping. We verify every payment before we continue. Payments are not refundable.

Where do I find my chat?

Tap the three-dot menu at the top right of the website. Choose My orders for order chats or Help for questions. A red dot means you have an unread reply.

How do I track my parcel?

We ship with India Post. When your parcel is dispatched, we send the tracking ID in your order chat. Tap Track on India Post to check it.

Login does not work when I open the site from Instagram

Google blocks login inside the Instagram or Facebook browser. Open the website in Chrome or Safari (tap the three dots and choose Open in browser), or use the Continue with email link option.

My card does not open the link

  • Make sure NFC is turned on in your phone settings (Android).
  • On iPhone, hold the top back of the phone against the card for a second.
  • Remove thick phone cases or metal plates.
  • Still not working? Open your order chat and send us a short video. Your card has a 3-year warranty.

Can I change the link on my card?

Yes. Message us in your order chat and we will guide you.
