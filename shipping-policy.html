Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. (Update this date whenever you change this page.)

1. How we ship

We ship all orders with India Post. When we hand over your parcel, we receive a tracking ID and we share it with you in your order chat. You can also see it in the order summary at the top of the chat.

2. When we ship

  • Your order is shipped after your address is confirmed, the required payment is verified, and your cards are programmed and tested.
  • For orders of 10 or more cards, the balance payment must be completed before we ship.
  • Delivery time depends on India Post and your location. We cannot promise an exact date.

3. Tracking your parcel

Open your order chat from the menu and tap Track on India Post next to your tracking ID. Paste the ID on the India Post tracking page to see where your parcel is.

4. Address and phone number

Please share a complete address with pincode and a working phone number. We are not responsible for delays caused by a wrong or incomplete address.

5. Delays, loss or damage

If your parcel is delayed, lost or damaged in transit, tell us in your order chat. We will help you raise a complaint with India Post using the tracking ID and will work with you to resolve the issue.

6. Shipping area

We ship across India. If you are outside India, please message us first.
